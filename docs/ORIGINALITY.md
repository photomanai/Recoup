# Recoup — Why this is different (Originality)

## The common solutions today

| Category | Examples | What they do | What they DON'T do |
|----------|----------|--------------|--------------------|
| **Uptime monitoring** | Datadog, PagerDuty, Better Uptime, Checkly | Page you when your site is down | Don't map incidents to SaaS SLA clauses, don't compute the credit you're owed, don't file the claim |
| **SaaS spend / cost management** | CloudHealth, Vantage, CloudZero | Track what you spend | Don't recover service credits you're contractually owed for downtime |
| **Manual SLA claim** | Excel + copy-paste email | Someone re-keys vendor status pages at month-end and writes a claim email by hand | Usually never happens — the whole point of SLA credits is that they're *claim-based, never automatic* |

## The gap Recoup fills

Every company paying Cloudflare has an SLA right to credits for downtime. The
vendor **never applies them automatically**. The only way to get them is to file
a claim within 5 business days with evidence. Nobody does this at month-end
because it's manual, rare, and easy to forget — which is *exactly why the
vendors keep that money*.

**Recoup is the only thing that connects all three dots:** monitoring → SLA
clause matching → AI-drafted, evidence-packaged claim filed to BOTH sides at
once.

## What's genuinely different (not just features)

1. **The claim is the product, not the alert.**
   Monitoring tools stop at "your site is down." Recoup starts there and
   continues to *"you are owed $X, here is the draft claim, here is the
   deadline, and here is both emails sent."*

2. **Dual AI-drafted emails.**
   One plain-language briefing to the finance/ops team (dollars + deadline),
   one formal, SLA-clause-referencing claim to the vendor. Two audiences, two
   tones, one incident — both generated by the AI pipeline.

3. **Deadline guard as a first-class citizen.**
   The 5-business-day Cloudflare claim window is modelled, stored per incident,
   and shown in the UI. Recoup never lets a claim silently expire — the core
   reason credits go uncollected.

4. **Fine-tuning roadmap → universal agent.**
   Today Cloudflare rules are explicit. Tomorrow, a fine-tuned model learns each
   vendor's formula/evidence/format directly from public SLA docs, so plugging
   in AWS, Twilio, Zendesk or Salesforce is data work, not engineering work.

## In one line

> Monitoring tools tell you the site is down. Recoup tells you the site is
> down **and files the refund you're owed for it — with AI writing both sides
> of the conversation.**
